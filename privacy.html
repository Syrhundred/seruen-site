Privacy Policy for Seruen

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Seruen is a small, private activity circle. You share steps and workouts with a handful of people you choose, and with nobody else.

This policy describes exactly what the app stores, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It is written to be checked against the app rather than skimmed — everything below is something the code actually does.

The short version

  • There are no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising of any kind in

Seruen. Not a reduced set — none.

  • Your health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold,

and never shared with data brokers.

  • Only people in a circle with you can see your activity, and you choose what

each circle sees.

  • You can export everything, or delete everything, from inside the app.

What Seruen stores

Your account

When you sign in with Google or Apple, Seruen receives your email address and, where the provider supplies it, your name and profile picture. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, Seruen only ever sees the private relay address Apple generates — it cannot see your real one.

You may also add a short bio and your own photo. Your device's time zone is stored so that a day ends at your local midnight rather than somewhere else's.

Seruen never receives or stores a password. Sign-in is handled entirely by Google or Apple.

Your activity

With your permission, Seruen reads from Apple Health (iOS), Health Connect (Android), or your phone's motion sensor:

  • daily step totals
  • distance, active minutes, active energy, and flights climbed,

where the source provides them

  • workouts that were recorded for you

Workouts you log by hand store their type, duration, intensity, start time, and any note you write. Your daily step goal is stored too.

Seruen does not read body measurements, weight, heart rate, sleep, blood glucose, reproductive health, or anything clinical. It does not request those permissions.

Your circles

Seruen stores which circles you belong to, your sharing preferences for each one, and the social activity inside them: feed items, reactions, comments, and encouragement ("nudges") you send or receive.

Notifications

If you turn notifications on, Seruen stores a push token for your device so it can tell you when someone encourages you or changes a goal you share. That token identifies the device, not you, and is deleted when it goes stale or when you delete your account.

Kept only on your phone

Reminder times, a cached copy of your recent numbers so the app works offline, and a note that you finished setting up. None of this is uploaded.

Who can see what

Other people in your circle see only what your sharing switches allow. Steps, workouts and active minutes each have their own switch, per circle, and you can change them at any time. Turning one off takes effect immediately.

Your step goal is visible to people in your circle. Your position relative to that goal is not published as a ranking — the leaderboard orders by steps and never shows how far short of a target anyone fell.

If a circle's creator sets a goal for you, you are notified, and you can change it yourself at any time.

Nobody outside your circles can see any of it. This is enforced in the database itself, not only in the app.

What you can do

Export everything. You → Export my data produces a file containing your profile, summaries, workouts, goals and circle memberships.

Delete everything. You → Delete account erases your profile, steps, workouts, goals, comments, reactions and nudges, and removes you from every circle. It cascades through the database and cannot be undone.

Change what you share, per circle, at any time.

Turn off health access in iOS Settings or Health Connect. Seruen will stop reading new data; it will not invent numbers to fill the gap.

Reporting and blocking

If someone in a circle behaves badly, you can report them from their member sheet. The report goes to us and names the reason you chose and anything you write — the person reported is never told who reported them. We review every report and act within 24 hours, which can mean removing content or removing an account.

You can also block someone. Blocking is mutual and silent: you stop seeing their posts, comments, reactions and encouragement, and they stop seeing yours. They are not told. You can undo it any time under You → Blocked.

Blocks are enforced by the database rather than hidden in the app, so a blocked person cannot reach your activity by any route.

How long it is kept

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting your account removes it immediately and permanently. Stale push tokens are removed automatically.

Age

Seruen is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is designed to be used by families, and younger members should set it up with a parent or guardian. Seruen does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the app will say so before the change takes effect.
